### Slide 8: Design Defined
**Design is the forming and rendering of intent.**

- **Forming intent:** Discovering the right problem and envisioning the right solution
- **Rendering intent:** Executing that vision with skill and craft

**Good design** = excellence in both forming AND rendering

**Bad design** = failure in either one

> Why did I have you discuss your definitions before sharing mine?

---

### Slide 9: Principles of Great Design & Learning
**If you want to be a great designer, be a great learner.**

- **Feedback reveals blindspots** — External perspectives show what you cannot see yourself
- **Externalization clarifies thinking** — Explaining your design helps you find gaps
- **Failure is natural** — First attempts are experiments, not final answers
- **Incubation enables breakthroughs** — Your brain works even when you step away
- **Iterate low to high fidelity** — Start simple, progress to complex
- **Teaching deepens understanding** — You don't fully know until you can teach it
- **Reflection accelerates growth** — Without reflection, experience doesn't become learning

### Slide 11: Key Concepts
**Three related ideas:**
- **Wayfinding:** Navigating through space — "How do I get from here to there?"
- **Journey Map:** Visualization of experience over time, showing touchpoints and emotions
- **Experience Map:** Broader than journey maps — captures any human experience
