### Class Flow

1. **Vector Flattening Demo** (~10 min)
   - Show how to combine multi-layer icons into a single vector object
   - Illustrator: Pathfinder > Unite, or Object > Expand
   - Figma: Flatten selection (Cmd/Ctrl + E)

2. **A4 Final Deliverable & Rubric Review** (~15 min)
   - Review A4 requirements:
     - PDF process book (sketches → finished work)
     - Final map on one page (default 17" x 11" horizontal)
     - 1-2 sentence concept statement
     - At least 1 caption describing a change made from feedback
   - Walk through the 5 rubric criteria
   - Clarify what "Exemplary" looks like

3. **Work Session + 1:1 Feedback** (remaining time)
   - Students finalize icons and map
   - Instructor meets individually with students
   - A3 due by end of class
