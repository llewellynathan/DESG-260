# AI Fishing Moves
## Eight reusable ways to direct AI when you don't know what you're doing

*Keep this handout. The tools will change. These moves won't.*

---

### 1. Teach Me, Don't Do It For Me
**Use when:** You don't understand the thing AI is about to do for you.
**Say:** *"Before you produce anything, explain the approach you're going to take, and why. Then ask me if I want you to proceed."*
**Why it works:** Forces AI to surface its reasoning before you commit. Gives you a chance to redirect early.

---

### 2. Give Me Trade-Offs, Not Answers
**Use when:** AI jumps to "the" answer and you suspect there's more than one way.
**Say:** *"Give me 2–3 ways to do this, with the strengths and weaknesses of each. Don't recommend yet."*
**Why it works:** The first answer is rarely the best one. Trade-offs reveal the shape of the decision you're actually making.

---

### 3. Predict Where I'll Get Stuck
**Use when:** You're about to start something unfamiliar.
**Say:** *"I'm new to this. Before we begin, tell me the three things most likely to confuse me, and what to do when I hit each one."*
**Why it works:** Pre-empts the rabbit holes you'd otherwise fall into. Turns AI into a map-maker, not just a worker.

---

### 4. Explain Like I'm a Design Student
**Use when:** AI drops jargon you don't recognize.
**Say:** *"Explain this to someone who's never seen code / never used this tool / has never heard this term. Define each word the first time you use it."*
**Why it works:** AI defaults to the vocabulary of whoever it thinks you are. Naming your audience re-anchors the whole conversation.

---

### 5. Diagnose My Confusion
**Use when:** AI just re-explained something and you *still* don't get it.
**Say:** *"I don't understand X. Don't re-explain — ask me questions to find out where my understanding actually breaks down."*
**Why it works:** Flips AI from monologue mode to diagnostic mode. Often the gap is one specific assumption — the question format surfaces it faster than more explanation.

---

### 6. Reject the First Answer by Default
**Use when:** AI produced something that works but feels more complicated than it needs to be.
**Say:** *"Is there a simpler way? What would you recommend if I wanted the minimum viable version, even if it's less flexible?"*
**Why it works:** AI tends to over-engineer. Asking for the minimum version is a forcing function for clarity, and you can always add complexity later.

---

### 7. Demand Opinions
**Use when:** AI gives you a list of options and waits for you to choose.
**Say:** *"Stop listing options. Pick one and defend it. Tell me when you'd recommend the others instead."*
**Why it works:** A list of options is AI hedging. A defended recommendation is AI's actual judgment — which is usually what you actually need.

---

### 8. Capture What We Decided
**Use when:** You've just had a useful conversation and don't want to lose it.
**Say:** *"Summarize what we just figured out, in a form I could paste into my notes and reuse tomorrow. Include the key trade-offs we considered and the decision we made."*
**Why it works:** Conversational AI output evaporates. A structured summary converts the conversation into a durable artifact you can share, reference, or show an instructor.

---

## Three rules for using the moves

1. **Move #1 is almost always your first move.** "Teach me before you do it" prevents 80% of the messes you'd otherwise clean up later.
2. **Never accept an answer you can't explain to a classmate.** If AI gave you code or a concept you can't re-state in plain English, you haven't finished the conversation. Use Move #4 or Move #5.
3. **Always end with Move #8.** The conversation disappears when you close the tab. The summary is what makes the learning durable.

---

## One anti-pattern to avoid

**Copy-pasting AI output into your work without reading it.** This is cognitive offloading, not delegation. Every minute you save by skipping the read-through, you pay back later when something breaks and you don't know what's in your own project.

If you don't have time to read what AI produced, you don't have time to ship it.
