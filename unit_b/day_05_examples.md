# App Examples for Project B: Make It Better

These apps are highly rated and widely used, but aren't household names like Instagram or Spotify. They're great candidates for the "Make It Better" project because they have clear purposes, active user bases, and room for UX improvement.

---

## By Category

### 🍳 Meal Planning & Recipes
- **Crouton** — Recipe collection & meal planning
- **Mealime** — Generates 30-min meal plans with smart grocery lists
- **Paprika Recipe Manager** — Recipe clipper & digital cookbook
- **Yummly** — Taste-based recipe recommendations

### 🌱 Plant Care
- **Planta** — Smart watering reminders using 30+ parameters
- **Greg** — Weather-adaptive plant care scheduling
- **Vera (by Bloomscape)** — Free, minimalist plant journal

### 😴 Sleep & Wellness
- **Sleep Cycle** — Smart alarm that wakes you during light sleep
- **SleepScore** — Sonar-based sleep tracking (no wearable needed)
- **Daylio** — Mood tracking with icons instead of typing
- **Reflectly** — AI-guided journaling prompts

### ✅ Habits & Productivity
- **Habitify** — Elegant habit tracker with category organization
- **TickTick** — To-do list + habit tracking combined
- **Loop Habit Tracker** — Free, open-source, privacy-focused (Android)
- **Streaks** — Apple Design Award winner, simple streak tracking

### 📚 Studying & Learning
- **RemNote** — Notes + spaced repetition flashcards in one app
- **Mochi** — Markdown-based flashcards with clean interface
- **Brainscape** — AI-enhanced collaborative flashcards

### 💰 Finance & Budgeting
- **Copilot** — Privacy-first budgeting (Apple only)
- **Monarch Money** — Made by former Mint developers
- **Goodbudget** — Digital envelope budgeting system
- **Lunch Money** — Clean UI budgeting for former Mint users

### 🚇 Transit & Travel
- **Transit** — Real-time vehicle tracking in 200+ cities
- **Citymapper** — Multi-modal journey planning
- **Moovit** — Covers 3,500+ cities globally

### 🩸 Health Tracking
- **Clue** — Science-first period tracking
- **Flo** — Popular period tracker (67M monthly users)
- **Bearable** — Symptom tracking for chronic conditions

### 👗 Secondhand Shopping
- **Vinted** — 34M users, 0% seller fees
- **Mercari** — Sell almost anything peer-to-peer
- **Carousell** — Popular marketplace, no listing fees

### 📔 Journaling
- **Diarium** — One-time purchase, Microsoft award winner
- **Grid Diary** — Question-based daily journaling
- **DailyBean** — Cute "mood beans" micro-journaling

## Apps with Known UX Pain Points

These apps have documented usability frustrations, making them interesting redesign candidates:

- **Workday** — Overly complex job application flows
- **Discord** — Notification overload by default
- **LinkedIn** — Irrelevant notification spam
- **Airbnb** — Hidden fees revealed late in booking flow
- **Amazon** — Cluttered product pages, buried information

---

## Remember: Selection Criteria

From the assignment brief:

- 💚 An app you actually use (or gave up on)
- 💚 Clear purpose it's failing to achieve
- 💚 Focus on one user flow, not the whole app
- ❌ Not already well-designed apps (Instagram, Spotify, etc.)
