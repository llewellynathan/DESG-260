# Day 8 — Wednesday, Feb 4
## Unit B: Make It Better App

**Focus:** The design process is iterative — discovery, definition, development, and delivery overlap.

**Targeted Learning Outcomes:** Better Interactions & Experiences, Detailed Documentation

---

### Desired Results

**Essential Questions:**
- EQ1: How do we know when a design is "done enough" — and how do we decide what to polish vs. what to cut?
- EQ2: What does it mean for a new feature to be indistinguishable from the original app?

**Understandings:**
- U1: The design process is iterative and non-linear. Discovery, definition, development, and delivery overlap and repeat.
- U2: A high-fidelity prototype must match the original app's design language in typography, spacing, color, and interaction patterns to feel native.
- U3: Documentation is a design activity — how you present your process and decisions matters as much as the final screens.

**Students Will Know:**
- K1: How to structure a documentation PDF with clear typographic hierarchy and cohesive layout
- K2: The six required sections of the B4 documentation (Title Page, User Flow, Competitive Analysis, User Task Diagrams, Paper Prototype + Iterations, High-Fidelity Final Prototype)
- K3: How to export and present Figma prototypes effectively

**Students Will Be Able To:**
- S1: Refine a digital prototype to match native app quality
- S2: Synthesize all project deliverables (B1-B3) into a cohesive documentation PDF
- S3: Identify and resolve remaining usability issues through peer feedback

### Activities

1. **Warm-Up: Testing Insights Share** (10 min) `[EQ1, U1, S3]`
   - Write: What was the most surprising thing users did with your digital prototype?
   - Share one insight with a neighbor
   - How did testing your digital prototype differ from testing your paper prototype?

2. **Today's Learning Goals** (5 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, U1, U2, U3, K1, K2, S1, S2]`
   - You've formed intent (research, user flows, competitive analysis) and begun rendering it (paper prototypes, digital prototypes, user testing)
   - Today: Bring it all together. Refine your prototype and begin assembling your documentation
   - Preview: B3 check-in, documentation walkthrough, then extended work session with 1:1 meetings
   - B4 due before Day 9 (Monday, Feb 9)

3. **B3 Digital Prototype Check-In** (15 min) `[EQ2, U2, S1, S3]`
   - Quick gallery walk: Everyone opens their Figma prototype on their laptop
   - Walk around, try 2-3 classmates' prototypes
   - For each: Does the new feature feel native? What breaks the illusion?
   - Provide brief written feedback (sticky note or message)

4. **Direct Instruction: Documentation Walkthrough** (20 min) `[U3, K1, K2, K3]`
   - **I do:** Walk through the six required sections of the B4 documentation PDF:
     1. **Title Page with Creative Brief** — Goal of the new interaction
     2. **User Flow of Existing App** — With critique (pros/cons)
     3. **Competitive Analysis** — Matrix + quadrant positioning chart
     4. **User Task Diagrams** — Existing flow + modified flow with visual separator
     5. **Paper Prototype + Iterations** — Show feedback, iterations, reasoning
     6. **High-Fidelity Final Prototype** — Device mock-ups, annotations, indistinguishable from native
   - Show 1-2 examples from previous semesters
   - Emphasize: The documentation itself should be well-designed. Clear typographic hierarchy, consistent grids, cohesive layout
   - The documentation illuminates the design — it does not clash with nor overshadow it
   - Practical tips: exporting from Figma, creating device mock-ups, writing annotations

5. **Work Session with 1:1 Meetings** (remaining ~130 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, U1, U2, U3, S1, S2, S3]`

   **What you're working on:**
   - **Build final high-fidelity prototype in Figma (no AI tools):** Polish interactions, match native design language, fix issues from testing. Your B4 prototype must be your own Figma work — use what you learned from AI in B3, but build this one yourself.
   - **Begin documentation PDF:** Start assembling your B1-B3 deliverables into the six-section format
   - **Add annotations:** Explain your design decisions on the high-fidelity screens
   - **Device mock-ups:** Place final screens into device mock-ups

   **Instructor circulating for 1:1s:**
   - Does your prototype feel native to the platform?
   - Are you building your final prototype in Figma without AI tools?
   - What feedback from testing did you act on? What did you decide not to change, and why?
   - Is your documentation telling a clear story of your design process?
   - Are all six sections accounted for?

   **B4 Due: Before Day 9 (Monday, Feb 9) on Learning Suite**
   - Complete documentation PDF with all six sections
   - Include link to Figma prototype

**Total time:** ~180 minutes
