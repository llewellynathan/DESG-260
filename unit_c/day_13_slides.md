### Slide 11: Mobile Typography Constraints
**The rules of small screens:**
- **Body text:** 16px minimum (anything smaller is unreadable)
- **Touch targets:** 44px minimum (fingers are imprecise)
- **Hierarchy:** Less variation needed — fewer sizes, more restraint

> Check your mobile wireframe: Is your body text at least 16px?

---

### Slide 12: Choosing and Pairing Typefaces
**Function first, personality second**

1. **Function:** Readability, x-height, weight options
2. **Personality:** Does this feel right for the brand/persona?

**Pairing strategies:**
- **Contrast:** Serif + sans-serif (creates tension, visual interest)
- **Harmony:** Same family, different weights (cohesive, subtle)

---

### Slide 13: Good vs. Bad Pairings
**What makes a pairing work?**
- Sufficient contrast OR clear harmony
- Shared proportions (similar x-height)
- Complementary personalities

**What breaks a pairing:**
- Too similar (why use two?)
- Clashing personalities
- Competing for attention

> Let's look at a student's type choices together: Does it work at mobile sizes? Does it fit the persona?

### Slide 16: User Testing Tips
**How to test effectively:**

1. Ask them to find specific information ("Where would you check shipping?")
2. Watch, don't guide — silence is okay
3. Note where they hesitate or get confused
4. Their confusion is data, not failure
