# Font Weight

> **Weight controls hierarchy—use it to emphasize and de-emphasize parts of the interface.**

## Components
1. **Font weight** — Actual thickness defined by the typeface (Light, Regular, Bold, etc.)
2. **Visual weight** — Perceived heaviness from space, color, and layout

## Essential Practices
- Use weight changes to signal meaning and hierarchy
- Never rely on faux bold—use fonts with real weights baked in
- Create emphasis with whitespace and color, not just bold
- Reserve heavy weights for headings, regular for body

## Quick Reference
- **Body copy**: Regular (400), Book, or Medium (500)
- **Headings**: Semi-bold (600), Bold (700), Heavy/Black (800-900)
- **Light/Hairline**: Only for large headlines—hard to read otherwise
- **Negative space**: Surround text with whitespace for emphasis without boldness
- **Color weight**: Contrasting backgrounds draw attention without heavy fonts
