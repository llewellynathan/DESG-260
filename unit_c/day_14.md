### Activities

1. **Warm-Up: Final Checklist Self-Review** (15 min) `[EQ2, K1, S1]`
   - Checklist on screen — students review their own work:
     - [ ] All required content included (price, reviews, shipping, etc.)
     - [ ] Mobile AND desktop versions complete
     - [ ] Grid alignment consistent throughout
     - [ ] Type hierarchy clear and consistent
     - [ ] Design reflects persona's priorities
   - Mark items that need attention before submission
   - Identify your top 2-3 polish priorities for today

2. **Today's Learning Goals** (5 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, U1, U2]`
   - This is our final Unit C class — C5 is due tonight
   - Today's focus: final polish and documentation
   - Connection to forming/rendering: you've *formed* your intent through persona research and testing; today is about *rendering* it with excellence
   - The goal: submit work you're proud of

3. **Quick Tips: Common Polish Issues** (10 min) `[K1, U2, S2]`
   - Brief walkthrough of common issues to fix:
     - **Alignment:** Elements not snapping to grid; inconsistent margins
     - **Spacing:** Uneven padding; cramped or floaty elements
     - **Type:** Inconsistent styles; orphans/widows; poor line length
     - **Responsive:** Content that doesn't make sense on mobile; touch targets too small
     - **Details:** Placeholder text still present; low-res images; missing states
   - These details are the difference between "student work" and "portfolio piece"

4. **Work Session: Final Polish + Documentation** (145 min) `[S1, S2, S3, EQ1, EQ2]`
   - **Priority 1:** Address issues from self-review checklist
   - **Priority 2:** Incorporate any remaining feedback from C4 testing
   - **Priority 3:** Compile process documentation:
     - Persona sheet + job-to-be-done
     - Typography stylesheet
     - Wireframes
     - Any saved iterations
   - **Priority 4:** Export final PDF (full page lengths, no cropping)
   - Instructor available for 1:1 feedback and troubleshooting
   - **Optional:** Guest discussion on BFA program applications (for those interested)

**Total: 175 min** (allowing 5 min buffer)
