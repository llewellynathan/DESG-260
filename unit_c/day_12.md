# Day 12 — Wednesday, Feb 18
## Unit C: Hierarchy & Responsive Web

**Focus:** Wireframes are thinking tools — they let you solve layout problems before committing to details.

**Targeted Learning Outcomes:**
- Better Interactions & Experiences
- Communication and Feedback
- Design Strategy

---

### Desired Results

**Essential Questions:**
- EQ1: Why wireframe before going high-fidelity?
- EQ2: How do you evaluate whether a layout is working before adding visual polish?
- EQ3: What feedback is most useful at the wireframe stage?

**Understandings:**
- U1: Wireframes separate structure decisions from visual decisions
- U2: Getting feedback early (on wireframes) saves time later
- U3: The same content requires different layout solutions on mobile vs. desktop

**Students Will Know:**
- K1: What makes an effective wireframe (clarity, hierarchy, no distracting details)
- K2: How to give constructive feedback on layout and structure

**Students Will Be Able To:**
- S1: Create clear wireframes that communicate layout intent
- S2: Give and receive feedback on wireframes
- S3: Iterate on layout based on peer feedback

### Activities

1. **Warm-Up: Wireframe Self-Assessment** (10 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, U1]`
   - Prompt on screen: "Open your wireframes. Can someone who's never seen your PDP understand: (1) What's the product? (2) Where's the price? (3) How do I buy it?"
   - Students annotate their own wireframes with checkmarks or question marks
   - Identifies gaps before peer feedback

2. **Today's Learning Goals** (5 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, EQ3, U1, U2, U3]`
   - Today's focus: getting your wireframes solid before going high-fidelity
   - Connection to forming/rendering: wireframes are about *forming* — solving the layout problem; high-fidelity is *rendering* — making it beautiful
   - We'll do peer critique, then you'll have extensive work time to finish C3

3. **Peer Wireframe Critique** (30 min) `[EQ3, U2, K2, S2, S3]`
   - Form groups of 3
   - Each person shares wireframes (mobile + desktop) for 10 min:
     - 2 min: Creator explains the persona and their priorities
     - 3 min: Group members try to "use" the wireframe (find price, reviews, shipping)
     - 5 min: Feedback discussion — what's working, what's confusing
   - Focus feedback on structure and hierarchy, not aesthetics
   - Note: This is about the layout, not the type choices or colors

4. **Direct Instruction: Responsive Layout Patterns** (20 min) `[U3, K1, S1]`
   - **I do:** Show common responsive patterns
     - Stacking (desktop columns → mobile stack)
     - Reordering (what's most important on mobile?)
     - Hiding/revealing (what can be collapsed on mobile?)
   - **I do:** Demo refining a wireframe based on feedback
     - Show a "before" wireframe with issues
     - Walk through making it clearer
   - **We do:** Quick exercise — sketch how one element of your PDP could reflow
   - Key question: Does your mobile layout put the most important content first?

5. **Work Session: Wireframe Refinement + High-Fidelity Start** (115 min) `[S1, S3, EQ2]`
   - **Priority 1:** Incorporate peer feedback into wireframes
   - **Priority 2:** Finalize C3 wireframes (due tonight @ 11:59pm)
   - **Priority 3:** If wireframes are solid, begin high-fidelity work
   - Instructor circulates for 1:1 feedback:
     - Is the hierarchy clear in the wireframe?
     - Does mobile prioritize the right content?
     - Are the desktop and mobile versions coherent (same system, different arrangement)?

**Total: 180 min**
