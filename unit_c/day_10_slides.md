### Slide 7: What is a Type Scale?
**A mathematical ratio between sizes**
- Common ratios: 1.25 (Major Third), 1.333 (Perfect Fourth), 1.5 (Perfect Fifth)
- Each step up multiplies the previous size by the ratio
- Creates natural, harmonious relationships between heading levels

> Demo: typescale.com — watch how changing the ratio affects hierarchy

---

### Slide 8: The Four-Size Rule
**Limit yourself to a maximum of 4 type sizes**
1. **Titles** (H1) — the main headline
2. **Subtitles** (H2/H3) — section headers
3. **Body** — paragraph text
4. **Metadata/Captions** — small supporting text

Size jumps should be at least 2px (14px vs 13px is too subtle to read as different)

**Create variety with color, weight, and case — not more sizes**

---

### Slide 9: Weight for Hierarchy
**Use weight to emphasize and de-emphasize**

| Context | Weight |
|---------|--------|
| Body copy | Regular (400) or Medium (500) |
| Headings | Semi-bold (600) or Bold (700) |
| Large headlines only | Light/Hairline (300 or less) |

Light weights are hard to read at small sizes — save them for display text only.
