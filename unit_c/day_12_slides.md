### Slide 5: Why This Matters
**Why this matters**
Jumping straight to high-fidelity means you're solving two problems at once: layout AND aesthetics.

Wireframes let you fail fast and cheap. Changing a gray box takes seconds. Changing a polished design takes hours.

Professional designers wireframe first because it's faster to throw away a sketch than a finished screen.

### Slide 8: Critique Structure
**How critique works**
- Groups of 3
- Each person gets 10 minutes:
  - **2 min:** Explain your persona and their priorities
  - **3 min:** Group members try to "use" the wireframe (find price, reviews, shipping)
  - **5 min:** Feedback discussion — what's working, what's confusing

> Focus on structure and hierarchy, not aesthetics. This is about layout, not colors or type choices.

### Slide 10: Common Responsive Patterns
**Three ways content adapts**
- **Stacking** — Desktop columns become mobile stacks
- **Reordering** — Most important content moves up on mobile
- **Hiding/revealing** — Some content collapses on mobile (expandable sections)

> Which of these are you using in your PDP?
