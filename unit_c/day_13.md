### Activities

1. **Warm-Up: Persona Priority Mapping** (10 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, U1, U2]`
   - Prompt on screen: "Look at your current PDP wireframe. Circle the 3 things your persona would care about most. Then star what's actually most prominent in your design."
   - Students mark up their wireframes (5 min)
   - Brief pair-share (2 min): Do your circles match your stars? What's the gap?
   - Sets up the discussion: are we designing for ourselves or for our persona?

2. **Today's Learning Goals** (5 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, EQ3, EQ4, U1, U2, U3, U4]`
   - Today's focus: everything you need to create excellent high-fidelity designs
   - We'll cover: empathy and ethics, typography choices, and the principles behind polish
   - Connection to forming/rendering: *forming* = deeply understanding your persona; *rendering* = making type, layout, and detail choices that serve them
   - By end of class: high-fidelity PDP in progress with the tools to make it great

3. **Discussion: Empathy, Attention, and Design Ethics** (10 min) `[EQ2, U2, K3]`
   - Facilitated discussion based on Jensen reading:
     - What does it mean to design for someone's attention responsibly?
     - How does your PDP either respect or exploit your persona's attention?
   - Connect to persona work: you're not designing for "users" — you're designing for *this person*
   - Design as an act of service, not extraction

4. **Direct Instruction: Typography for Mobile + Type Pairing** (15 min) `[K1, K2, U3, S1]`
   - **I do:** Mobile typography constraints
     - Minimum readable sizes (16px body minimum)
     - Touch target considerations (44px minimum)
     - How hierarchy changes on smaller screens (less variation needed)
   - **I do:** Choosing and pairing typefaces
     - Function first: readability, x-height, weight options
     - Personality second: does this feel right for the brand/persona?
     - Pairing strategies: contrast (serif + sans) or harmony (same family)
   - **We do:** Quick evaluation of a type choice — does it work at mobile sizes and fit the persona?

5. **Direct Instruction: Why Polish Matters — Gestalt Principles** (15 min) `[EQ4, K4, U4, S4]`
   - Quick intro: Gestalt psychology explains how humans perceive visual relationships
   - Six principles that apply to UI design:
     - **Proximity:** Elements close together are perceived as related → why spacing matters
     - **Similarity:** Elements that look alike are perceived as related → why consistency matters
     - **Continuity:** Elements in a line/curve are perceived as connected → why alignment matters
     - **Closure:** We perceive incomplete shapes as complete → simplify icons and graphics
     - **Common Region:** Elements in a bounded area are grouped → why cards and containers work
     - **Common Fate:** Elements moving together are related → why coordinated animations matter
   - These aren't arbitrary rules — they're based on how human perception works
   - When polish is off, it creates cognitive friction users can't articulate but definitely feel

6. **Quick Tips: Common Polish Issues** (10 min) `[K5, U4, S4]`
   - Brief walkthrough of issues to watch for (with Gestalt context):
     - **Alignment (Continuity):** Elements not snapping to grid; inconsistent margins
     - **Spacing (Proximity):** Uneven padding; cramped or floaty elements
     - **Type (Similarity):** Inconsistent styles; orphans/widows; poor line length
     - **Responsive:** Content that doesn't make sense on mobile; touch targets too small
     - **Details:** Placeholder text still present; low-res images; missing states
   - These details are the difference between "student work" and "portfolio piece"
   - You now have the *why* behind these rules — use it as you build your designs

7. **Introduce C4: High Fidelity V1 + Testing** (5 min) `[S2, S3]`
   - **Due: Feb 23 @ 11:59pm** (tonight!)
   - Requirements:
     1. High-fidelity designs for both desktop AND mobile
     2. User testing with at least 2 people using your own script
     3. Submit PDF with: hi-fi designs + testing summary + what you plan to change
   - Quick tips for user testing:
     - Ask them to find specific information ("Where would you check shipping?")
     - Watch, don't guide
     - Note where they hesitate or get confused

8. **Work Session: High-Fidelity PDP** (50 min) `[S1, S2, S3, S4, EQ1, EQ4]`
   - **Priority 1:** Move from wireframes to high-fidelity designs
   - **Priority 2:** Apply refined type choices (mobile-appropriate sizes)
   - **Priority 3:** Check your work against Gestalt principles — is spacing consistent? Is alignment clean?
   - Instructor circulates for 1:1 feedback:
     - Is type readable at mobile sizes?
     - Does the layout serve the persona's priorities?
     - Are design decisions intentional and defensible?
