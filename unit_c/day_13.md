# Day 13 — Monday, Feb 23
## Unit C: Hierarchy & Responsive Web

**Focus:** Design for the person, not the screen — empathy drives every layout decision.

**Targeted Learning Outcomes:**
- Better Interactions & Experiences
- Problem Discovery & Research (Curiosity, Empathy, Humility)
- Communication and Feedback

---

### Desired Results

**Essential Questions:**
- EQ1: How does understanding your user change what you prioritize in a layout?
- EQ2: What does it mean to design with empathy and attention?
- EQ3: How do you choose typefaces that serve both function and personality?

**Understandings:**
- U1: Content should drive design decisions — the persona determines what matters most
- U2: Empathy in design means anticipating needs, not just responding to requests
- U3: Typography choices communicate personality while serving readability

**Students Will Know:**
- K1: Strategies for choosing and pairing typefaces
- K2: How mobile constraints affect type size, touch targets, and layout
- K3: The relationship between attention, agency, and ethical design

**Students Will Be Able To:**
- S1: Refine type choices based on persona and platform constraints
- S2: Adapt layouts thoughtfully between mobile and desktop
- S3: Articulate design decisions in terms of user needs

### Activities

1. **Warm-Up: Reflection on Reading** (10 min) `[EQ2, U2, K3]`
   - Prompt on screen: "From the reading — what does 'attention' have to do with design? Write 2-3 sentences connecting Jensen's ideas to your PDP project."
   - Students write independently
   - Brief pair-share (2 min): discuss with a neighbor
   - Sets up the discussion: design as an act of caring for the user's attention

2. **Today's Learning Goals** (5 min) `[EQ1, EQ2, EQ3, U1, U2, U3]`
   - Today's focus: refining your PDP with empathy and intention
   - Connection to forming/rendering: *forming* = deeply understanding your persona; *rendering* = making type and layout choices that serve them
   - We'll discuss attention and agency, then refine type choices for mobile
   - By end of class: high-fidelity PDP in progress, ready for user testing

3. **Discussion: Empathy, Attention, and Design Ethics** (15 min) `[EQ2, U2, K3]`
   - Facilitated discussion based on Jensen reading:
     - What does it mean to design for someone's attention responsibly?
     - How does your PDP either respect or exploit your persona's attention?
     - What's the difference between persuasion and manipulation?
   - Connect to persona work: you're not designing for "users" — you're designing for *this person*
   - Design as an act of service, not extraction

4. **Direct Instruction: Typography for Mobile + Type Pairing** (20 min) `[K1, K2, U3, S1]`
   - **I do:** Mobile typography constraints
     - Minimum readable sizes (16px body minimum)
     - Touch target considerations (44px minimum)
     - How hierarchy changes on smaller screens (less variation needed)
   - **I do:** Choosing and pairing typefaces
     - Function first: readability, x-height, weight options
     - Personality second: does this feel right for the brand/persona?
     - Pairing strategies: contrast (serif + sans) or harmony (same family)
     - Show 2-3 good pairings and 1-2 bad ones
   - **We do:** Evaluate a student's current type choices together
     - Does it work at mobile sizes?
     - Does it fit the persona?
     - What would make it stronger?

5. **Introduce C4: High Fidelity V1 + Testing** (10 min) `[S2, S3]`
   - **Due: Feb 23 @ 11:59pm** (tonight!)
   - Requirements:
     1. High-fidelity designs for both desktop AND mobile
     2. User testing with at least 2 people using your own script
     3. Submit PDF with: hi-fi designs + testing summary + what you plan to change
   - Quick tips for user testing:
     - Ask them to find specific information ("Where would you check shipping?")
     - Watch, don't guide
     - Note where they hesitate or get confused

6. **Work Session: High-Fidelity PDP + Peer Feedback** (60 min) `[S1, S2, S3, EQ1]`
   - **Priority 1:** Move from wireframes to high-fidelity designs
   - **Priority 2:** Apply refined type choices (mobile-appropriate sizes)
   - **Mid-session:** Quick peer feedback round (10 min)
     - Pair up, show mobile designs
     - Partner tries to find: product price, shipping info, reviews
     - Note any confusion points
   - **Priority 3:** Begin user testing outside of class (2 people required)
   - Instructor circulates for 1:1 feedback:
     - Is type readable at mobile sizes?
     - Does the layout serve the persona's priorities?
     - Are design decisions intentional and defensible?

**Total: 120 min**
