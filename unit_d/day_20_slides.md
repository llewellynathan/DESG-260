### Slide 8: Brand is a system
**Visual identity is a system of decisions**
- Typography creates hierarchy and scannability
- Color creates meaning and draws attention
- Spacing creates rhythm and breathing room
- Consistency builds trust — inconsistency feels broken

The question isn't "What looks cool?" — it's **"What serves this person?"**

> Show 2–3 apps in the same category with different visual identities. What does each communicate about who the product is for?

---

### Slide 9: Typography hierarchy
**A hierarchy is a system of roles**
- **Display** — App name, splash screens (largest, most expressive)
- **Heading** — Section headers, screen titles (clear, bold, scannable)
- **Body** — Descriptions, content (readable, comfortable)
- **Caption/Label** — Metadata, timestamps (small, unobtrusive)
- **Button** — Interactive elements (medium weight, high contrast)

Every size needs a purpose. If you can't name its role, you don't need it.

---

### Slide 10: Type pairing
**Pairing typefaces**
- Contrast: pair a serif with a sans-serif, or a geometric with a humanist
- Don't pair fonts that are too similar — it looks like a mistake
- One expressive font + one workhorse font is usually enough
- Consider your persona: playful vs. professional, warm vs. clinical

**Building a type scale:**
- Start from body text (16–18px on mobile)
- Use a ratio (1.25 or 1.333) to derive sizes
- Assign a role to each level

> Open your phone's Settings app. How many distinct type styles can you count? What role does each serve?

### Slide 12: Using AI for brand
**AI generates — you evaluate**
- AI tools: Claude for strategy, Coolors for palettes, Fontjoy for pairings, Figma Make for mockups
- Good prompt: "Generate 5 color palettes for a [adjective] [adjective] [product type] aimed at [persona]"
- AI can't: understand your specific users, evaluate cultural context, feel the emotional weight of a color

**Evaluating AI output:**
- Does this palette have sufficient contrast? (4.5:1 minimum for text)
- Does this type pairing match your brand personality?
- Would your persona trust this visual language?
- Is this distinctive or generic?

> Live demo: prompt AI for 3 brand directions for a sample app. Which best serves the persona? Why?
